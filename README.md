# FNF Supra 360

Fullscreen Android 3D showcase for the supplied orange Fast & Furious-style Supra GLB.

## Behavior
- Starts directly in the 3D scene.
- No visible controls or interface.
- Camera stays at a fixed 3/4 front showcase angle.
- The vehicle root is continuously rotated through a full 360 degrees.
- The camera was moved farther back and slightly higher for a wider view.

## Model
The supplied `tractor.glb` is already included at `app/src/main/assets/tractor/tractor.glb`.


## Showroom behavior
The camera is fixed at a slightly elevated three-quarter view while the GLB root rotates on the +Y axis at 12 degrees/second. A 1.3-second black transition is triggered at each 90-degree presentation boundary.
